To my sister

A note for you.

Because a birthday deserves more than just a text message.

Hey sis,

Happy birthday! I hope today gives you a reason to smile, a moment to pause, and the kind of happiness that stays with you long after the candles are blown out.

Growing up together gives us a collection of memories that nobody else could quite understand. Some are funny, some are chaotic, and some are the small everyday moments that end up meaning the most. I’m grateful for all of them, because I got to share them with you.

I don’t always say it out loud, but I notice the way you keep going, the way you care, and the way you bring your own light into a room. I hope you see in yourself what the people who love you see so easily.

I hope this year brings you new memories, little adventures, big laughs, and everything you’ve been quietly wishing for. You deserve so much good in your life.

Take photos, eat the cake, laugh too loudly, and let yourself enjoy being celebrated today. And when the day is over, remember that I’m proud of you on the regular days too.

No matter how old we get or where life takes us, I’ll always be cheering for you. Happy birthday. 💖

With love,
unnyy
